Art History: Media of Expression

Topic Description

Art is the expression of human emotion into physical forms of media. I believe spreading information on the arts will help keep them alive and flourishing for generations to come. Art often gets reduced to just paintings and sculptures in museums, but there is so much more than that. There are countless forms of media that people use to express themselves throughout history. From the paintings and sculptures we talked about to textiles and carpentry, there is always going to be a medium found for humans to express themselves. I want to help spread awareness to these lesser-known areas so they get the appreciation they deserve.

People have and will always need a form of expression, and art is the outlet for that. Some of the earliest evidence we have of human civilization is the art that people left behind, whether that was cave paintings, carved figures, or decorated tools. Long before written language, humans were already communicating through images and objects, continuing to show how deeply embedded the urge to create is.

In a capitalistic world where nearly everything we make is tied to profits or productivity, art remains one of the few things people do solely for themselves. It does not have to serve a function or produce a profit; it just has to have meaning to the person making it. Art is not only a record of skill and technique but also gives us a glimpse into what these cultures felt was worthy of expression.

App Description

Landing Page

The landing page will combine images with information about the topic above. Since cave paintings are among our first evidence of art and among the first images visitors will see on the site, they will be used as the opening visual to introduce both the website and the art world itself.

How the App Works

Visitors will move through a simple two-step sequence to explore different media of artistic expression:

  1. Choose one of four broad categories of art.
  2. Choose one of four specific media within that category.
  3. View images and a description of that specific medium.

Step 1: Choose a Category

Visitors will first choose from four major categories of artistic media:

Painting & Drawing

  • Oil Painting
  • Acrylic Painting
  • Watercolor
  • Graffiti

Sculpture & 3D Media

  • Stone Carving
  • Bronze Casting
  • Ceramics
  • Woodworking

Textiles

  • Weaving
  • Embroidery
  • Quilting
  • Macramé

Print

  • Woodblock Printing
  • Lithography
  • Etching/Engraving
  • Photography

Step 2: Choose a Medium

After selecting a category, visitors will choose one of the four specific media listed under it. Each selection will display a few images along with a description explaining what the medium is and its history.

Further Reading Page

A final page will go into further detail on the human need for expression and how the arts satisfy that need, tying the specific media back to the themes introduced on the landing page.
