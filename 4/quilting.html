Quilting

What it is

Quilting is the art of sewing layers of fabric together to make a warm, padded textile. A quilt usually has three layers: a decorated top, a middle layer of padding called batting, and a plain backing. Stitches run through all three layers to hold them in place, and those stitches often form patterns of their own. Many quilt tops are patchwork, made by sewing together small pieces of fabric in colorful designs. Quilts are used as bed coverings and wall hangings.

A brief history

Quilting is an old technique. Padded and stitched fabric was used for clothing and armor in medieval Europe, where it gave warmth and protection. Settlers brought quilting traditions to North America, and many families made quilts from leftover scraps of fabric, which helped patchwork become a popular folk art. Quilts also became a way to record stories and mark events, such as the quilts made by the women of Gee's Bend, Alabama, and the AIDS Memorial Quilt. Today quilting is practiced as both a craft and a fine art.
