Ceramics

What it is

Ceramics is the art of shaping clay into objects and hardening them with heat. Artists form clay by hand, on a pottery wheel or in molds, then fire it in a kiln so it becomes hard and permanent. Many pieces are also covered in a glaze, a glassy coating that adds color and makes the surface smooth and waterproof. Ceramics covers everything from everyday bowls and cups to decorative vases and sculpture.

A brief history

Fired clay is one of the oldest art materials, with small figurines and pottery vessels made by people tens of thousands of years ago. Ancient Greek potters decorated vases with painted scenes, and Chinese potters developed porcelain, a fine, white ceramic that was traded around the world. Pottery traditions grew up independently in many cultures, and ceramics remains popular today as both craft and fine art.

Quick facts

  • Often finished with a colored glaze
  • Shaped by hand, on a wheel or in molds
  • Made for both everyday use and decoration
