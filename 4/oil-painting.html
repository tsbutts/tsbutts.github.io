Oil Painting

What it is

Oil paint is made by mixing pigment with a drying oil, most often linseed oil. It dries slowly, so artists can blend colors on the canvas and make changes over several days.

A brief history

Oil painting became a major medium in Europe in the 1400s, and Flemish painters were among its early masters. Its slow drying time and rich color made it popular for portraits and large scenes for centuries.

Source: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Heilbrunn Timeline of Art History.

Quick facts

  • Surface: canvas, wood panel or paper
  • Dries slowly, often days or longer
  • Cleaned up with solvents or oils
  • Known for rich, blendable color
